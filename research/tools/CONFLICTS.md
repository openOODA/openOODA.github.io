# TOOLS process conflicts (and solutions)

Process lenses can fight product goals or each other. Owner-visible summary.

| ID | Fight | Problem | Solution | Owner? |
|----|-------|---------|----------|--------|
| **T1** | \(S\downarrow\) vs feature ship | Features raise \(U\) until tests land | Ship **with** pass+fail rails; \(S\) is ship gate, not feature ban | N |
| **T2** | E-M thrust vs DESIGN/PM research | Vision papers don’t lower \(S\) immediately | Separate **vision credit** (PM/research) from **entropy ship** (TOOLS) | N |
| **T3** | Power law ≤5 vs full PM checklist | Sprint cannot touch all DESIGN leaves | SPRINT top-5; PM tracks all; power law is *per turn* | N |
| **T4** | Dual-engine \(D\) vs pure-only product | \(D\) term may be stale | Redefine or retire \(D\) when pure path is sole truth | **Y** |
| **T5** | Science metaphor overclaim | Readers treat \(S\) as real thermo | Every TP paper states **proxy / analogy limits** | N |
| **T6** | VoI “smallest probe” vs deep research | Agents skip DESIGN/TP papers | VoI for *product claims*; research series has its own cadence | N |
| **T7** | STE100 vs research density | Full STE can block precise research prose | STE for ops/TOOLS/residuals; structured density OK in RP/TP | N |
| **T8** | Homeostasis vs hard residuals | “Reduce load” used to avoid free/ARC work | Homeostasis rare; residuals stay on PM | **Y** if abused |
| **T9** | Assembly depth vs pure-host marketing | Seed+C is real TCB | Honest TCB layers (see DESIGN CONFLICTS C9) | N |

## Default resolution stack

1. **Product truth** (ooda residual docs + tests)  
2. **PM.md** DESIGN status  
3. **TOOLS** entropy/E-M for *this turn’s* ship  
4. **Research papers** justify DESIGN/TOOLS claims—never override fail-closed product
