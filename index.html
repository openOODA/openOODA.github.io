Why openOODA? The Boyd doctrine

In air combat, systems work, and AI-assisted coding, advantage goes to whoever closes the decision cycle faster — not who has the biggest peak specs.

1. Velocity over peak power

Col. John Boyd’s OODA loop framed victory as operating inside the opponent’s decision cycle. openOODA applies that to compilers: short observe → orient → decide → act loops for humans and agents.

2. Guard rails, not ceremony

Capabilities and contracts make incorrect I/O and broken promises hard to ship, without requiring a separate security product bolted on later.

3. Designed for AI co-authoring

Compact modules, machine-readable errors, and token-light outlines shrink the loop between “agent edited code” and “toolchain said no — here’s why.”
